Who updates
Each owner updates their own rows. The Founder's Office checks the whole tracker daily.
When
Every Monday by 11 am, and the same day anything changes.
Status
Green on track. Amber at risk, under 3 days of buffer, or waiting on someone. Red past its end date or making a dependent task late.
Date moves
